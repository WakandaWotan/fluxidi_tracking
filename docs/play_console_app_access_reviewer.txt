Fluxidi — Google Play Console “App access” instructions (English)

Package: com.fluxidi.tracking
Build: Google Play Internal testing (consumption-only company SaaS)

--------------------------------------------------
REQUIRED DEMO ACCOUNTS (create outside source control)
--------------------------------------------------

Create durable, non-expiring demo credentials (no SMS/OTP). Store passwords
only in Play Console App access / a secure password manager — never in git.

1) Company admin (required)
   Role: company owner/admin
   Purpose: business home, bookings, drivers/vehicles, subscription STATUS
            page (read-only entitlements; no in-app SaaS purchase on Play),
            Chiron/compliance surfaces as available for the demo tenant.

2) Driver (required)
   Role: driver linked to the demo company
   Purpose: active trip / navigation / metering / ride payment surfaces
            (Mollie online, QR, cash, Tap to Pay, invoice where configured).

3) Customer (recommended if customer booking UI is reviewed)
   Role: customer account able to open a booking with the demo company
   Purpose: customer booking + tracking views.

Do NOT require one-time SMS codes unavailable to Google reviewers.
Prefer email+password or a fixed company PIN already provisioned.
